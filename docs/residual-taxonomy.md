# Residual Taxonomy

## 1. Definition

A residual is any resource, observation, byproduct, or unrealized value
produced during an inference cycle that was not consumed by the primary
output path.

A residual is not automatically waste.

Waste is a residual for which no safe and legitimate next connection
has been established.

## 2. Residual categories

### rejected_candidate

A candidate answer, route, hypothesis, or action that was generated but
not selected.

Possible future value:

- alternative planning,
- boundary-condition discovery,
- counterfactual analysis,
- fallback execution.

### intermediate_result

A partial computation produced before the final result.

Possible future value:

- cache reuse,
- incremental computation,
- agent handoff,
- repeated-query acceleration.

### failed_attempt

An execution or reasoning path that did not achieve its intended result.

Possible future value:

- failure prevention,
- test generation,
- recovery policy,
- operational training.

### unused_evidence

Evidence retrieved or generated but not used in the final conclusion.

Possible future value:

- later verification,
- dispute resolution,
- alternate analysis.

### discovered_constraint

A limitation, boundary, incompatibility, or prohibited condition
identified during inference.

Possible future value:

- routing policy,
- authorization policy,
- safety rules,
- planning constraints.

### safety_observation

A security, reliability, privacy, or alignment-related observation.

Possible future value:

- detection rules,
- quarantine policies,
- human review,
- threat intelligence.

### cache_candidate

A result that may reduce repeated computation.

Possible future value:

- local caching,
- retrieval acceleration,
- energy reduction.

### idle_capacity

Unused computational, temporal, network, or operational capacity.

Possible future value:

- low-priority tasks,
- Trace organization,
- compression,
- verification.

### thermal_byproduct

Heat generated by physical computation infrastructure.

Possible future value:

- building heating,
- water heating,
- industrial heat recovery.

### unallocated_value

Economic value that has not yet been assigned to a beneficiary.

Possible future value:

- royalty allocation,
- shared funds,
- maintenance reserves,
- community infrastructure.

### other

A residual not covered by the standard categories.

The summary and extension fields should explain its meaning.

## 3. Residual forms

Residual category and residual form are separate concepts.

The category explains why the residual exists.

The form explains what kind of resource it is.

Supported forms:

- data,
- computation,
- thermal,
- temporal,
- economic,
- operational.
